1. Who this is for

Santa Call is directed to children aged 3–12 and their parents or legal guardians. A parent or guardian must set up the app and supervise each conversation. The interface and conversations are English only.

2. What we collect

  • Parent account information: the Apple user ID and email address shared through Sign in with Apple (which may be an Apple private relay address), plus agreement and authorization records.
  • Child profile information: nickname or name (up to 60 characters), exact age from 3 to 12, gift wish, achievement, parent notes or guidance, and topics to avoid. These details are saved on the device and sent through our API to OpenAI to personalize a call. This is not limited to an age band. [VERIFY AFTER NICKNAME/AGE-BAND CHANGE]
  • The child's live microphone voice/audio during an accepted call.
  • StoreKit purchase, refund, minute balance and usage history. Apple handles payment details; we do not collect payment card numbers.
  • Login IP address and time, and technical diagnostics such as app version, error category, timestamps, and account or call identifiers.

3. How voice works

After a call is accepted and microphone access is allowed, the child's live voice is streamed in real time via WebRTC directly from the device to OpenAI's Live API (model gpt-live-1) to generate the AI Santa voice. Audio does not pass through or get stored on our own server. We do not store recordings or conversation transcripts in the app or our API. Profile context passes through our API separately from the audio stream.

This describes Santa Call's processing, not OpenAI's retention practices. OpenAI may retain data under its own policy for safety monitoring. 4. Who receives data

  • OpenAI receives live audio and profile context as our AI voice provider.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and StoreKit purchases.
  • Google Cloud hosts our API, account and billing database, service logs and backups.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

5. Parental consent and parental rights

A parent signs in with Apple and must accept the current data disclosure before proceeding. Microphone permission and a fresh supervision confirmation are required for an accepted call. These actions acknowledge processing; they do not establish verified parental consent. The verified parental authorization process required before child use has not launched. Sandbox's simulated authorization is solely for adult testing with fictional details. [TODO: CONFIRM VERIFIED PARENTAL CONSENT PROCESS BEFORE CHILD USE]

We will need to verify the requester's authority before disclosing or deleting data. Do not include a child's sensitive information in the initial email.
